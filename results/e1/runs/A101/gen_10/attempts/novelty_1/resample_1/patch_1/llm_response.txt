<DESCRIPTION>
Cooperate initially, then make a single probe against opponents that have never defected. If they retaliate once, forgive to restore cooperation; if they defect repeatedly, switch to defection. This preserves mutual cooperation with reciprocal opponents while exploiting unconditional cooperators.
